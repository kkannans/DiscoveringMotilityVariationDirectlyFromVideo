Discovering organoid motility variation directly from video

Each point is one of 108 organoids. The video-prediction model is trained leave-some-out — across 10 splits, so every organoid is held out (in the test set) at least once — and prediction surprise is how hard the model, trained on the rest of the population, finds that held-out organoid's motion to predict. Click a point to watch its prediction versus reality. High surprise = it moves unlike the rest.
